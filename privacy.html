Privacy policy

Preview template. This page ships with every Tiyan Digital build so the legal slot is never empty — the show's lawyer replaces it with final wording before launch.

What we collect

  • Contact and newsletter forms: your name and email address, only what you type in, only to reply or send episode emails.
  • Nothing else. This demo sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads no third-party trackers. Fonts are hosted on this site itself.

Why we collect it

To answer your message and to send the episode emails you asked for. Nothing else. We never sell data, and we never share it with advertisers.

Your rights

Ask us anytime to show, correct, or delete your data — email the show and it happens. Unsubscribing from episode emails takes one click and deletes your address.

How long we keep it

Contact messages: as long as the conversation needs, then deleted on request. Newsletter addresses: until you unsubscribe.

Children

This site is for a general audience. If you're under 16, please have a parent or guardian contact us on your behalf.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears here with an updated date.
