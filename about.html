● About the show

School, minus the boring parts.

School2Podcast started with a simple observation: the most useful lessons rarely happen during the lesson. Sample

So the show goes looking for them — in staff rooms, dorm halls, late-night study sessions, and the habits of people who figured things out the hard way. Each week: one idea from the world of learning, talked through honestly, with someone who has lived it. Sample

No lectures. No jargon. Just the conversation you wish someone had recorded the first time you needed it. Sample

What the show covers

  • Study habits
  • Teacher stories
  • Student life
  • Life skills
  • Exams & stress
  • Career first steps

Why a website at all?

A podcast lives on other people's platforms — which means the show doesn't own the relationship with its listeners. This site fixes that: every episode searchable, every transcript readable, every listener one click from subscribing on their app, and an email list the show actually owns.
